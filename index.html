About PGM Lube PTE. LTD

PGM Lube PTE. LTD is a Singapore-based marine lubricant supplier specializing in high-performance lubrication solutions for the global maritime and offshore industry.

We are committed to delivering reliable, technically advanced, and application-specific lubricant products that support the operational efficiency, safety, and longevity of marine engines and critical onboard systems.

Operating from Singapore—one of the world’s leading maritime hubs—PGM Lube leverages strong industry connectivity, technical expertise, and stringent quality standards to serve ship operators, fleet managers, and offshore service providers across international waters.

Our product portfolio includes marine engine oils, hydraulic fluids, gear oils, greases, and specialty lubricants engineered to meet demanding operational conditions and OEM performance requirements.

At PGM Lube, we focus on consistency, compliance, and customer trust. Every solution we deliver is backed by technical understanding, quality assurance, and a commitment to supporting uninterrupted marine operations worldwide.

We continue to strengthen our presence as a dependable partner in the marine lubrication sector through innovation, service reliability, and long-term customer relationships.
