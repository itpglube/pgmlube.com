About Us

PGM Lube is a dedicated brand of PetroGulf, specializing in the development and supply of advanced marine lubricants. Built on decades of industry knowledge, our products are designed to meet the demanding needs of ships and offshore operations worldwide.

We combine technical expertise, rigorous quality standards, and a customer-first approach to deliver lubricants that ensure performance, reliability, and long-term protection. From engine oils to hydraulic and specialty products, every solution is engineered to withstand the challenges of marine environments while helping our partners optimize efficiency and reduce downtime.

At PGM Lube, we don’t just supply lubricants — we deliver confidence, innovation, and trusted support to keep your fleet moving.
